Privacy policy

Last updated: October 28, 2026.

What data we use

  • From your Google account: your email, name and profile picture. We only use them to identify you when you sign in.
  • What the organizer of an exchange types: each participant's name and, if they add it, their Google email; plus which Google account is linked to each name.
  • What you add to your wishlist: gift name, cost, link, photo and order of preference.
  • The result of the draw: who each participant is giving a gift to.

Who can see what

  • Each participant only sees who they're giving a gift to. Nobody, not even the organizer, can see anyone else's assignment.
  • Your wishlist is only visible to the person who got you.
  • Registered emails and which account is linked to each name are only visible to the organizer, so they can fix mistakes.

What we use it for

Only to make the exchange work. We don't sell or share your data, we don't show ads and we don't use tracking tools.

Where it's stored

The app is hosted on Vercel and the data is stored in a Neon PostgreSQL database. We use a single cookie, needed to keep you signed in. Your browser stores your chosen language and the last account you signed in with, to offer “Continue as…”; “Use another Google account” removes it.

Deleting your data

The organizer of an exchange can remove you from it or delete it entirely, along with wishlists and the draw. You can also ask us to delete your data by writing to CONTACTO@EJEMPLO.COM.
